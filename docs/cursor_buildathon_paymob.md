About Event
​Join us for the Cursor Cairo Buildathon, co-hosted by Cursor and Paymob, on 15 August 2026, from 1:00 PM to 8:00 PM, at Founders Spaces in Downtown Cairo.

​This is a hands-on build day for developers, designers, founders, product managers, students, and builders who want to turn a challenge into a working product using Cursor and Paymob’s payment tools.

​Apply Individually

​Applications are individual only. Please do not register as a pre-formed team.

​Selected participants will be organized into balanced teams during the event based on their skills and experience.

​You also do not need to submit or prepare an idea in advance. The organizers will provide the challenge areas, problem statements, and project ideas that teams will choose from and work on during the buildathon.

​What to Expect

​The day will include:

​Check-in and networking
​A short introduction to Cursor workflows and AI-assisted development
​An overview of Paymob’s payment tools and integration process
​Challenge announcement and team formation
​A focused build sprint with support from Cursor community mentors and the Paymob team
​Final project demos in front of the judging panel
​Winner announcements and networking
​What Every Participant Receives

​Every selected participant will receive:

​Cursor credits to use during the buildathon
​Support with Cursor setup and development workflows
​Technical and onboarding support from the Paymob team
​A dedicated Paymob onboarding contact to help take their project live
​Up to EGP 50,000 in fee-free Paymob transaction volume for their own project after the event
​Food and refreshments throughout the day
​Prizes

​Prizes are awarded to each member of the winning teams:

​First Place

​EGP 250,000 in fee-free Paymob transaction volume
​$500 in Cursor credits
​Second Place

​EGP 150,000 in fee-free Paymob transaction volume
​$250 in Cursor credits
​Third Place

​EGP 100,000 in fee-free Paymob transaction volume
​$100 in Cursor credits
​Who Should Apply?

​This event is suitable for:

​Software developers and engineers
​Designers and product builders
​Technical founders
​Product managers working closely with engineering teams
​Students and early-career builders with strong technical or product interests
​Anyone interested in building and shipping real products with Cursor
​You should be ready to collaborate with new people, contribute to a team, and build under time constraints.

​What to Bring

​Please bring your laptop, charger, any adapters you may need, and a valid Cursor account.

​Registration

​Capacity is limited, and registration is application-based.

​Submitting the form does not guarantee attendance. Applications will be reviewed individually, and selected participants will receive a confirmation email before the event.

​Do not register multiple people in one application and do not apply as a team. Each participant must submit their own application.

​Paymob benefits are subject to onboarding and eligibility requirements. Final credit distribution and benefit terms will be shared with confirmed participants.
